# Product Vision

## Vision statement

Make every hotel room a connected service point. Guests can request what they need with one scan and a few taps; hotel teams receive clear, actionable, room-specific work in one operational system.

## Problem

Hotels commonly coordinate simple guest requests through calls, paper registers, verbal handovers and messaging apps. This creates avoidable friction:
- Guests do not know which number to call or which service is available.
- Reception may need to relay requests to several departments.
- Requests can be forgotten, duplicated or assigned ambiguously.
- Managers lack a reliable view of open work and response performance.
- Room readiness, cleaning and guest occupancy can be tracked in separate places.

## Solution

A multi-tenant SaaS platform with:
1. Hotel/property/floor/room administration.
2. A unique, configurable QR experience for every room.
3. Guest self-service for Wi-Fi information and hotel services.
4. Real-time staff request routing, assignment and status updates.
5. Role-based staff and management workspaces.
6. Check-in/check-out and housekeeping coordination.
7. Operational reporting and SaaS subscription administration.

## Primary personas

- **SaaS operator:** creates and supports hotel tenant accounts, plans and platform settings.
- **Hotel owner:** configures properties, staff, services, plans and performance.
- **Hotel manager:** supervises daily operations and escalations.
- **Receptionist:** handles guest support, stays, room assignment and reception requests.
- **Housekeeping staff:** works on cleaning and amenity tasks.
- **Room-service staff:** fulfils water, food and other delivery requests.
- **Maintenance staff:** resolves room equipment and facility issues.
- **Guest:** scans the room QR and requests services without installing an app.

## Product principles

- Guest experience: minimal steps, readable text, accessible touch targets.
- Operational clarity: every request has a room, category, status, timestamps and accountable owner.
- Tenant isolation: no cross-hotel data leakage.
- Configurability: hotels can enable services, departments, labels, hours and branding.
- Reliability: persist requests before acknowledging success; realtime is an enhancement over durable records.
- Privacy: collect the minimum guest information needed.
- Mobile-first: guest and staff workflows must work well on phones.
- Incremental delivery: a usable MVP before advanced integrations.

## Success measures

- Percentage of active rooms with a working QR code.
- Guest QR scans and service requests per occupied room.
- Median and 90th percentile time to acknowledge and complete requests.
- Percentage of requests completed within each hotel's configured service target.
- Number of missed, duplicated, reopened and escalated requests.
- Staff adoption and daily active staff users.
- Hotel activation, retention and subscription conversion.
- Support tickets per hotel and QR failure rate.

## Product positioning

A hotel guest-service and operations platform, not merely a QR menu or booking website. It can later integrate with a full PMS, payment gateway, restaurant POS, smart locks or third-party booking channels.
