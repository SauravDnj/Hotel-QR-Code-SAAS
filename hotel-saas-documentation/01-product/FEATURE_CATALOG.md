# Feature Catalogue

## Guest QR portal
- Hotel logo, name, welcome message and room label.
- Wi-Fi network name and password with tap-to-copy.
- Hotel facilities, restaurant timings, check-in/check-out times, policies and contact details.
- Service tiles controlled by hotel administrator.
- Water bottle / drinking water.
- Housekeeping / room cleaning.
- Extra towels, pillows, toiletries and amenities.
- Reception call/request.
- Maintenance issue (AC, TV, plumbing, lighting, Wi-Fi).
- Room service / dining (MVP request-only; full ordering later).
- Laundry pickup (configurable).
- Emergency contact instructions.
- Feedback and rating.
- Guest-safe request history/status.

## Staff workspace
- Live request queue with sound/visual alerts.
- Filter by property, floor, room, department, priority, status and age.
- Accept, assign, reassign, start, complete, cancel and reopen.
- Internal notes and guest-visible response separation.
- Task history and timestamps.
- Staff availability/on-duty state (later).
- Escalation and overdue indicators.
- Mobile PWA installation support.

## Reception and room operations
- Room grid and list.
- Occupancy status.
- Check-in, room assignment, expected checkout and checkout.
- Guest/stay record.
- Arrivals and departures list.
- Checkout triggers housekeeping task.
- Room inspection and ready-for-sale state.
- Block room for maintenance.
- Reservation calendar in a later phase.

## Hotel administration
- Hotel profile and branding.
- Properties, floors, room categories and rooms.
- QR generation and bulk print sheets.
- Services, service options, departments and routing.
- Staff, roles, permissions and property scope.
- Wi-Fi and hotel information.
- Notification and escalation settings.
- Audit log and data export.

## SaaS administration
- Tenant onboarding and status.
- Plans, limits, trials and subscriptions.
- Feature flags and plan entitlements.
- Usage metering (active rooms, staff seats, properties).
- Platform-wide support view with audited, time-limited access.
- Billing provider webhook handling.
- Tenant suspension and reactivation.

## Suggested service configuration fields
- Name, description, icon, category.
- Enabled/disabled.
- Department route.
- Optional fields (quantity, option, note).
- Availability schedule.
- Guest-facing acknowledgement text.
- Priority and target response time.
- Whether guest progress is visible.
- Whether manager escalation is enabled.
