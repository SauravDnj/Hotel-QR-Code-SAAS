# Prompt 02 — Property, Floor and Room Management

Read PRD, database design, RBAC and workflows. Implement property, floor, room category and room management.

Include:
- Create/read/update/archive properties, floors, categories and rooms.
- Unique room code per property.
- Room list/grid with search, filters and pagination.
- Operational status separate from occupancy and housekeeping status.
- Permission and tenant/property scope checks.
- Audit history for sensitive changes.
- Validation, empty/loading/error states.
- Migrations and unit/integration/E2E tests.

Do not implement QR or guest requests yet. Verify cross-tenant and cross-property denial.
